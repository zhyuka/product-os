name: ambition
description: "Rewrites a prompt into a deeper version of the task, not a bigger one. Use for '/ambition', 'make this more ambitious', 'deeper version of this', 'what am I not asking for'."
---

I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one. Before rewriting it: (1) say what a top professional in this field would do here that I'm not asking for; (2) name the rawest material they would start from instead of what has already been digested for me; (3) name the specific methods and tools they would use; (4) say what, other than you or me, would show the result is right. Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that.

Keep the rewrite to the one thing my prompt asks for. Every step in it serves that deliverable: reading the material it is made from, the method, the check that it's right. A step that changes something else, such as another document, a process, a tool, who receives it or a message to someone, makes the task bigger, not deeper, so leave it out of the rewrite. If one matters, name it in one line after the rewrite as a separate ask. When you don't know what the thing holds or who uses it, make reading it the rewrite's first step instead of designing around a guess.
